These creatures we all sketched many years ago as a free drawing exercise. Watching a comfort show and drawing something that flows has been a fun pastime of mine. The names are silly and made up of sounds the kids made up.

I tried to stick to a limited color palette in this zine. Maybe this would be a fun outline to have for coloring pages.

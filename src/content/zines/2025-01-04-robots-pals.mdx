The robots in this zine were all drawn many years ago in one night. It was a free flow drawing exercise and I was testing new drawing tools. Focusing on the tools helped the characters come to life without overthinking any of the details. Some of these were used in the first simple animations I made.

Here are the animation I made with these characters.

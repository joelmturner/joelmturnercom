I was trying to make investing options less scary for myself so I decided to draw some the entities as cute characters. This didn't make investing any more clear but it did help fe feel better about it.

In this zine we have a chart, a bitcoin character, a gold bar, some candle chart candlesticks, a stack of silver coins, a money bag, and a piggy bank. Each of these characters are personified and drawn in rubber-hose style.

I often get the itch to put pen to paper and draw something but I don't have something in mind to draw. In these cases, I start with some small thing and keep drawing random things all around it, filling up the page as I go. The goal is to dance with positive and negative space so it all looks like something that goes together.

The individual drawings on the page aren't necessarily good by themselves, but when the page is filled, they look cohesive.

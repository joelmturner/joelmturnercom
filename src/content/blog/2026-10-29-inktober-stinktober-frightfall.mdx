Another October has passed and many illustrations have been made in participation with [#Inktober](https://www.instagram.com/explore/search/keyword/?q=%23inktober2026), [#Stinktober](https://www.instagram.com/explore/search/keyword/?q=%23stinktober2026) by [Chris Piascik](https://www.instagram.com/chrispiascik/), and [#Frightfall](https://www.instagram.com/explore/search/keyword/?q=%23frightfall2026) by [Retro Supply Co](https://www.retrosupply.co/).

I have been participating in [Inktober since 2017](https://joelmturner.com/illustration/?collection=inktober2017) and have started to incorporate/combine prompts with [Stinktober since 2024](https://joelmturner.com/illustration/?collection=inktober2024) which has mainly been done with a Sakura Pigma FB brush pen and Koh-I-Noor colored pencils.  

This was my first year doing Frightfall from Retro Supply Co. I completed them all using Adobe Fresco using brushes from Retro Supply Co. 

My favorite brushes for these drawings were the [1957 Atomic Ink from Inferno Ink](https://www.retrosupply.co/products/inferno-ink-for-photoshop?_psq=infern&_psid=163ac762f), and [Classic Inker from The Chris Piascik Pack](https://www.retrosupply.co/products/the-chris-piascik-pack-photoshop-fresco?_psq=chris+pi&_psid=12fcb0fc0).
